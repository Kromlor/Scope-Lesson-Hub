SCOPE LESSON HUB: QUICK START
=============================

OPEN THE APP
1. Keep this whole folder together. The pictures, voice clips, and lessons are in the folders next to index.html.
2. Double-click index.html. Use Chrome or Edge, and not a private/incognito window.
3. Always open it from the same folder on the same laptop. Records are saved inside this browser on this laptop.
   They do NOT sync to other computers, and nothing is sent over the internet.

FIRST LESSON: INTRO SLIDES ("Introducing SCOPE", pre-lesson)
- It is the first card in the library, and the "New to SCOPE? Start here." box at the top opens it.
- Present slides: shows 15 slides on the big screen. Click the slide or press the Right arrow to go on.
  Answers appear on the next click. Left arrow goes back. N shows teacher notes. F is full screen. Esc leaves.
- Start: runs the same content as cards with one or two students, with turns and ratings, like any lesson.
- The slides work offline. The editable PowerPoint version is in the slides folder (Meet-SCOPE.pptx).

RUN A LESSON (one or two students)
1. On Lessons, search or pick a unit, then press Start on a lesson card.
2. Choose students (add nicknames or initials right there), the part (Core lesson or Additional practice 1/2),
   Full or Short, who goes first, and the turn-taking method.
3. The colored banner says whose turn it is, for example "Jordan, your turn.", with a numbered circle
   or square for each student, so it never relies on color alone.
4. Students tap answers. Nothing moves on until YOU press "Save and next turn".
5. Quick check cards need a rating: Independent, With help, or Incorrect (or press Skip).
   A correct tap is not the same as independent, so you always choose the rating.
   Other cards can stay unscored, or you can rate them if you want.
6. SLP buttons: Back, Repeat this turn (keeps the first try), Switch answering student, Skip, Pause.
7. At the end, check each student's summary, add notes, and press Save session.
   If the laptop restarts or the page refreshes, the session picks up where you left off.

TYPED ANSWERS ("Check my answer")
- On open questions students type an answer and press "Check my answer" (or Enter).
  Right: "Yes!"  First miss: "Try again" + a hint.  Second miss: the sample answer shows.
- The checker looks for key words and allows small spelling slips. It can miss a good answer worded
  another way, so your panel has "Count as correct". You still choose the rating.
- Questions with no single right answer (personal questions) just say thanks. Talk-it-through cards stay spoken.
- Students can still answer out loud. To turn typing off: Settings > Answer boxes.
- Change a card's key words in the editor ("Check my answer" key words), and try an answer there first.

TWO STUDENTS
- Alternate questions: students take turns on different cards. "Talk it through" cards are shared and not scored.
- Both practice each skill: each student gets a turn on every card. If a card has a second-student
  question, the second student gets it. If not, both answer the same question and the answer stays
  hidden until both have answered. Then press "Show the answer".

MAKE OR CHANGE A LESSON
- Press Edit on a lesson card, or "+ Add lesson".
- Fill in title, unit, skill, the student goal, and your notes. Add a video link if you want one.
- Add cards. Each card has one question. For answer choices, check every answer that is acceptable.
  You can add a picture (SCOPE scenes or your own upload), hints, sentence starters, a sample answer,
  tone cards, your own recording of the question, and a second-student question.
- Your edits are kept as a draft while you work, even if the browser closes. Press Save lesson when done.
- Check "Ready to use" so the lesson can start. Problems are listed at the top until they are fixed.
- Archive hides a lesson; find it under "Archived" to restore it. Duplicate makes a copy to change.

BACK UP (do this often)
- Backup > Download backup. The .zip has lessons, students, sessions, and uploaded media.
  It contains student records, so keep it somewhere private (for example district OneDrive or SharePoint).
- To move to another laptop: copy this folder, open index.html there, Backup > Restore, and choose the .zip.
  The file is checked first. Nothing changes unless it is complete and you confirm.

RESULTS
- Session history shows every saved session. Open one to fix a rating or note.
- "Download results CSV" gives one row per answer; "Download summary CSV" gives one row per student per
  session. Both open in Excel or Google Sheets.
- Percentages use only Independent + With help + Incorrect items. Skipped and Not scored items are
  counted separately and left out. Each summary says what the percent is out of.

READ-ALOUD
- Speaker buttons read text aloud. Original SCOPE lesson 1–8 questions play the warm-voice recordings (376 clips).
- Other text (including all of lessons 9–60 and the pre-lesson) uses the computer voice, which is labeled. The app picks the most
  natural voice on the computer automatically; Microsoft Edge has the best ones. You can pick a voice in Settings.
  You can upload your own recording for any card in the editor.
- Cards labeled "SLP models the tone" need you to say the line. Hold "how to say it" in the SLP bar to peek.

SAMPLE MEDIA
- The sample-media folder has the Maya and Alex audio files you sent. To use one, open a lesson in the
  editor and add it as lesson media or card media ("+ Audio file").
